This policy applies to the Auxilium website and the Auxilium iOS app. We respect your privacy and only collect what we need to run Auxilium.

Information We Collect

  • Account. If you sign up, we collect your email address and a password. Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication.
  • Content you post. Events or articles you publish, including optional location text you enter in Where.
  • Push notifications. If you allow notifications, we store a device token so we can send alerts (for example a welcome message, or a reminder if you tap Remind me on an event). You can refuse or later disable notifications in iOS Settings.
  • App and site use. On the web we may use cookies or local storage to keep you signed in. The iOS app stores a sign-in session on the device.

Firebase and other services

Auxilium uses Google Firebase to operate the product:

  • Firebase Authentication for login and accounts
  • Cloud Firestore to store articles, events, and notification tokens
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (with Apple Push Notification service on iPhone) to deliver notifications you have allowed

Those services process data on our behalf so Auxilium can function. We do not sell your personal information.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To show and publish community content
  • To send push notifications you have opted into
  • To keep you signed in and improve Auxilium

Your Rights

You can log out or permanently delete your account in Auxilium: open Account (or Login) in the footer, then choose Log out or Delete account. Deleting your account removes your login. Events or articles you published may remain as community content. You can also email privacy@auxilium.guide.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with the updated date.
